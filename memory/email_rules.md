# Email Response Rules

## Who to respond to
- Respond to real people writing directly to me
- Skip newsletters, marketing, order confirmations, shipping notifications
- Skip emails where I am only CC'd — only respond if I am in the To: field
- Skip anything from myself (my own email address)

## Tone & style
- Professional but friendly
- Concise — match the length of the original email
- Never start with "I hope this email finds you well"
- Sign off as: [YOUR NAME]

## Common scenarios

**Meeting or call requests**
- Check calendar for availability before drafting
- Suggest 2 specific time slots

**Questions I can answer**
- Answer directly and briefly
- If unsure, say so honestly

**Follow-up or status requests**
- Acknowledge receipt, give a brief status update

**Unknown sender / cold outreach**
- Politely ask what they need and how they found me

## Do NOT auto-draft a reply for these — just alert me on Telegram
- Anything marked URGENT or time-sensitive
- Legal, contract, or financial emails
- Complaints or negative feedback
- Anything that feels sensitive or unusual

## Hard skip — do not notify me at all
- Password reset and security alert emails
- Automated receipts and invoices
- Social media notifications
- Any email with "unsubscribe" as the primary action
